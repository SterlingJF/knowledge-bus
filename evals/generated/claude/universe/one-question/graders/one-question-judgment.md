PASS if one answer settles the question and every clause narrows that same ask. A question whose single answer is a list, such as "Which items does the volunteer record?", asks one thing.
FAIL if the question joins two or more asks, or adds a second ask for a reason, a condition or a status, as in "Which teacher cancels the trip, and why?".
