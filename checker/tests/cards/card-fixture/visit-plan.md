# Visit plan

Document type in the Card fixture universe spec (card-fixture 0.1).

- Also called: site visit plan / risk briefing
- Helps you: prepare a safe site visit
- Who uses it: whoever leads the visit
- When it is used: before the visit
- No document needed when risk is low and audience is team. Depends on: How costly is a mistake on this visit? (risk); not stated in the universe spec (audience)

## How to write it well

- Heuristic: Keep the plan to one page.
- Heuristic, when familiarity is new: Add directions from the nearest public road. Depends on: How well does the reader know the site? (familiarity)

## Sections

### Site summary

Answers: What is the site and why are we visiting?
Required.

### Hazards

Answers: What could harm someone on the site?
Required when risk is high; otherwise optional. Depends on: How costly is a mistake on this visit? (risk)
Applies only when risk is high. Depends on: How costly is a mistake on this visit? (risk)

- Heuristic, when risk is high and familiarity is new: Mark each hazard on a map. Depends on: How costly is a mistake on this visit? (risk); How well does the reader know the site? (familiarity)

### Access terms

Answers: On what terms may we enter the site?
Required.
Kept in another document (Site permit): link to that document.

### Contacts

Answers: Whom do we call on the day?
When applicable, available when audience is public. Depends on: not stated in the universe spec (audience)
Kept in another document (Site permit): link to that document. The notes below are for writing the section in that document.

- Heuristic: Give a phone number that works on site.

### Costs

Answers: What did the visit cost?
When applicable.
Kept in another document: link to that document.

## How to present it

Factors change presentation and focus only. The sections stay the same.

### Card fixture universe spec (card-fixture 0.1)

#### Familiarity

Asks: How well does the reader know the site?

Values:

- new: Is the reader visiting the site for the first time?
- regular: Has the reader visited the site before?

Advice:

- Heuristic, when familiarity is new: Explain each local place name the first time it appears. Depends on: How well does the reader know the site? (familiarity)

## What the conditions mean

- risk is low: Can a mistake be fixed the same day?
- risk is high: Could a mistake harm someone or the site?
- familiarity is new: Is the reader visiting the site for the first time?
