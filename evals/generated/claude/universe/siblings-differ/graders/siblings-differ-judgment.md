PASS if each text tells the reader something its sibling does not, even when their topics touch or one case could be discussed under both; and where the wording is parallel, the changed words mark a real difference.
FAIL if the two texts are near-synonyms or one adds nothing the other lacks, or if parallel wording presents a difference that is not real. A case that fits both texts is never on its own a fail.
