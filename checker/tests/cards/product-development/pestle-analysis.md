# Pestle analysis

Document type in the Product development universe spec (product-development 0.9).

- Its shape is set by an outside authority: PESTLE
- Helps you: check which political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental conditions affect the work
- Who uses it: whoever sets the plan
- When it is used: before setting the plan, and again when laws, markets or technology change
- No document needed when time-separation is none and reversibility is reversible. Depends on: Who has to reconstruct this reasoning without the author present? (time-separation); Can this step be undone, and at what cost? (reversibility)

## Sections

### Macro environment factors

Answers: Which political, economic, social, technological, legal or environmental conditions affect the work?
Required.

- Convention: Six lenses: political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental.
- Heuristic: Weight by industry — legal and political dominate banking and healthtech, environmental dominates construction and energy, social and technological dominate consumer.
- Refresh: Review it after an election, a new law or regulation, or a change in the platforms or technology the work depends on.
- Heuristic: Where a condition fixes a limit the work must meet, record it in Constraints too.
- Empty: For a lens that was checked and holds nothing that affects the work, write 'nothing found', so a reader can tell it apart from a lens nobody checked.

### External opportunities threats

Answers: Which outside opportunities and threats matter to the work?
When applicable.
Kept in another document (Swot analysis): link to that document. The notes below are for writing the section in that document.

- Boundary: Distinct from Macro-Environment Factors, which lists outside conditions lens by lens; this judges which of them, and which competitor moves, help or threaten the work. The same law may appear in both: once as a fact, once as a threat with an owner.
- Heuristic: Draw each opportunity and threat from the PESTLE scan or the competitor profiles, and say which one it came from.
- Pitfall: A threat with no owner and no trigger gets noticed only once it lands. Carry each threat that needs a response into Risks & Assumptions, where it gets an owner.
