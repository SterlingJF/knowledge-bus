PASS if the text describes its subject as it currently is, including what it contains, what it is for, how it relates to other things, what kind or variant of another thing it is, the name it is known by even when that name carries a person or another work, when it goes out of date or what to do when something changes.
FAIL if the text tells something that happened to the subject in the past, such as a change from an earlier form, a rename, a split, an amendment, who founded it, what inspired it, or a contrast with how it was or was once thought to be.
