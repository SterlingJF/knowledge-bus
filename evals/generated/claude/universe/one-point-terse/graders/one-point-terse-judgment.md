type: llm
focus: last_message
---

PASS if the text makes one point in plain words and nothing in it could be cut without losing meaning. Short material that applies that same point is part of it, not a second point, whether it is a caveat, an exception, an instruction, examples, or the reason the point holds. A plain list of the items or traits the point covers passes, and so do short linked parts that together state one idea, such as questions that frame one subject.
FAIL if the text adds a second point that stands on its own, such as where the subject is standard, where it comes from, a second instruction that does not apply the first, or a separate observation about another case or a later effect; stacks two or more qualifiers that narrow when, which or how the one point applies; or keeps words, repeats or compound nouns that could be cut or shortened without losing meaning.
