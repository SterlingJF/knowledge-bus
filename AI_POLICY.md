# AI Policy

AI tools are welcome. You are responsible for everything you post here: code, wording, eval examples and comments. Understand everything you submit.

**Agents never commit, push, open issues or pull requests, or post comments on your behalf.** You do each of these yourself.

Write the problem in your issue and the "In Your Own Words" section of your pull request yourself. Write your replies to maintainers in your own words.

If you paste AI output, put it in a quote block, name the tool, keep it short, and add a line on why it matters.

You may write in your own language and add a machine translation below it in a quote block. If AI polishes your English, check that it still says what you mean.

Say how you used AI in the pull request's "AI Use" section. Write "None" or name each tool and what it did.

Eval examples must come from real cases. AI can help you restate and de-identify a case. It must not invent one.

Agents follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). It lists the commands they run and what they leave to you.

**Maintainers may close an issue or pull request that appear in violation of this policy, perhaps without notice..** Closing is not a judgement of you or your idea. Fix the problem and ask for it to be reopened.
