# Site permit

Document type in the Card fixture universe spec (card-fixture 0.1).

- Its shape is set by an outside authority: County site access permit
- Helps you: enter the site with the owner's permission
- Who uses it: whoever leads the visit
- When it is used: before the visit
- Not used when audience is team. Depends on: not stated in the universe spec (audience)
- No document needed when risk is low and audience is team. Depends on: How costly is a mistake on this visit? (risk); not stated in the universe spec (audience)

## How to write it well

- Convention: Use the county's form as issued.

## Sections

### Access terms

Answers: On what terms may we enter the site?
Required.

### Contacts

Answers: Whom do we call on the day?
Required.

- Heuristic: Give a phone number that works on site.

## What the conditions mean

- risk is low: Can a mistake be fixed the same day?
