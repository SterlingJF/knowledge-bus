# Counter hours memo

Date: 2026-02-23
From: office manager
To: department head

With one clerk, the counter cannot stay open from 8:00 to 16:00.
Options:

1. Keep full-day hours with a second clerk. The 2026 budget has no money for a second clerk.
2. Open the counter on weekday mornings only, with one clerk.
3. See applicants by appointment only. Phone booking takes the clerk away from the counter, and the online booking system arrives in 2027 at the earliest.

Recommendation: option 2.

Approved by the department head, 2026-02-25.
