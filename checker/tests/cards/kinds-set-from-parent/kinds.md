# Document types

## Committee minutes universe spec (committee-minutes 0.1)

Covers: What the management committee records at each meeting.

For: The committee secretary and the trustees.

- Minutes (minutes)
  - Helps you: record what the committee agreed
  - Who uses it: secretary
  - When it is used: during each meeting

## Hall upkeep universe spec (hall-upkeep 0.1), from the parent folder

Covers: The cleaning, checks and repairs that keep a village hall fit for each hirer.

For: The hall caretaker, the management committee and the people who hire the hall.

- Hirer checklist (hirer-checklist)
  - Helps you: leave the hall ready for the next hirer
  - Who uses it: hirer
  - When it is used: at the end of each event
